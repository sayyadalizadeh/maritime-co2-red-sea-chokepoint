# Table notes: supplementary analyses after the second pre-submission review (RS-D42)

Supplementary tables prefixed `stab_rev2_`; final numbers are assigned when the text is written. Values at full precision. Excess is in Mt CO2 over 2023-12..2025-10 unless stated.

- `stab_rev2_gross_exclusions.csv` (36 rows): RS-D42(a): gross gains and losses (% of the summed counterfactual and Mt) of TER_INT, RES_TOTAL and TER_TOT, S1 and S2, with EGY, SAU, MYS left out singly and jointly; maxima of the RS-D31 equal-window placebo gains and losses and the rank of the actual value (largest first). TER_TOT has no equal-window placebo runs. Source: `outputs/tables/review2_gross_exclusions.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
- `stab_rev2_multiple_testing.csv` (272 rows): RS-D42(b, c): per non-control unit of RES_TOTAL, TER_INT and RES_INT: RS-D31 p_u, Benjamini-Hochberg and Benjamini-Yekutieli adjusted p-values and candidates (q = 0.10), family-wise p-values (S1, S2, combined) and candidates (<= 0.10), smallest attainable family-wise p-value. Source: `outputs/tables/review2_multiple_testing.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
- `stab_rev2_multiple_testing_summary.csv` (3 rows): RS-D42(b, c): per series, the BH, BY and family-wise candidate lists and the smallest attainable family-wise p-value. Source: `outputs/tables/review2_multiple_testing_summary.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
- `stab_rev2_familywise_dates.csv` (74 rows): RS-D42(c): M_d, the maximum |standardized placebo statistic| over the non-control units at each equal-window placebo date, per series and specification, and the unit attaining it. Source: `outputs/tables/review2_familywise_dates.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
- `stab_rev2_r17_economies.csv` (247 rows): RS-D42(d) R17: per economy, the results with GIB, PAN, BHS and MHL removed from the control pools (columns as the main units files, plus principle). Source: `outputs/tables/review2_r17_economies.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
- `stab_rev2_r17_summary.csv` (4 rows): RS-D42(d) R17: removed pool members, pool sizes, totals with the placebo band, gross gains and losses (S1), robust list and changes against R0, with the R0 values beside. Source: `outputs/tables/review2_r17_summary.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
- `stab_rev2_rq3_hq.csv` (30 rows): RS-D42(e): RQ3 residence regressions (weighted, S1 and S2) with HQ as in RS-D3, HQ + HKG, and HQ + HKG + JPN: coefficients, HC3 standard errors, 95% intervals, R2 and n. Descriptive. Source: `outputs/tables/review2_rq3_hq.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
- `stab_rev2_w6.csv` (22 rows): RS-D42(f) W6 (non-inferential): container ships and the ten cargo types, estimation sample 2021-07..2025-10; S1 and S2 excess with the W0 values beside; placebo 2022-12 (data to 2023-11); aggregate pre-trend test for container ships on the same sample, with the main-sample test as reference. Source: `outputs/tables/review2_w6.csv` (src/26_review2_checks.py).
